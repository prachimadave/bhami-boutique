Our Roots & Heritage

The Story Behind Bhami Craft Boutique

Born in Pune, the cultural capital of Maharashtra, Bhami Craft Boutique was founded with a sacred purpose: to celebrate, revive, and elevate the timeless beauty of Maharashtrian wedding and celebration traditions.

Every Mundavalya is stringed with authentic Basra-style pearls, every Sakharpuda tray is hand-embossed in rich velvet with Mor-Pankh motifs, every Name Reveal Board is lovingly laser-carved, and each Customized Outfit & Bridal Blouse is meticulously stitched by master tailors. We believe that family milestones are sacred, and the artifacts blessed during these rituals should be heirloom treasures to cherish forever.

Ritual Authenticity

True to Maharashtrian Vedic traditions and auspicious colors.

100% Women-Led Crafting

Empowering skilled female artisans across Pune, Solapur & Kolhapur.

Heirloom Packaging

Protected in keepsake boxes designed to preserve your memories.
